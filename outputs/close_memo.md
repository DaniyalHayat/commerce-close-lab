# October accounting close review

## Decision

## Ownership and follow-up
Payments operations: investigate processor fees and missing settlements. Treasury: resolve duplicate bank records and deposit discrepancies. Inventory accounting: obtain missing item cost and reconcile purchase invoices to receipt evidence. Controller: review unresolved items, any correcting entries, and cutoff before approval. These are proposed responsibilities in a case study.

## Policies and boundaries
All inputs are synthetic. This is an illustrative first-party retailer, not an Amazon ledger. Capture records a processor receivable and customer advance; revenue and sales tax are recognized at shipment under the stated simplified transfer-of-control assumption. Refunds are full-order and returned goods are assumed resalable. Real policies require actual contract and delivery terms. Processing fees are expensed separately, never netted against first-party revenue. Settlement journals use processor-reported expected net cash; independent bank differences remain open, not plugged. Matched received invoices debit inventory and credit AP; disputed invoices and unreceived goods remain explicit exceptions. Opening inventory/equity are seeded balances, not current-period sales. No FX, partial shipments/refunds, intercompany, sales-tax remittances, inventory write-down reserve, chargebacks or tax provision is modeled. Missing COGS means profit is provisional.
